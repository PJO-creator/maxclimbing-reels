## 2. What we've learned so far

- **Hypothesis (Pieterjan, 19 Nov): regular reels beat trial reels.** B2 (121k) and Elbow (14.9k) were regular; PPS B/C were trials. Hard to prove: good trials get converted to regular, so the comparison is biased. Mechanism is plausible (regular = followers give the first engagement signal). Working rule: proven formats → regular; trials only for real A/B tests (e.g. two hooks), then post the winner as regular.
- **Gear choice is not a hook (Train at Home ep. 1, 30% view rate).** "Which setup do you prefer?" is a buyer's question that almost no scroller has right now. First frame = calm hang in the shop + 4 lines of text + a question before anything happened. Compare the elbow reel: same channel, problem the viewer feels in their body → 67%. Rules: (1) one idea per reel, (2) first frame = something happening or a felt problem, max ~8 words on screen, (3) products are the tool inside a protocol, never the topic, (4) don't polish a concept for hours before the first 3 s are proven: test the hook first.

- **Hook rate tells the story.** All PPS reels hold 69–72% past 3 s; Entralpi 56%, rankings 46–55%. Start with a recognisable action + a goal.
- **Shares drive the big one.** B2 got 893 shares (others: 0–149). Its caption opened with "Send this to the friend who's been almost there for 2 years". Use a share prompt aimed at a specific friend.
- **Saves follow protocols.** B2 1,494 saves, elbow 370, B 80, C 87 vs rankings 6–22, Entralpi 9, ice axes 1–2.
- **Goal hooks beat method hooks.** "How to train for a one-arm pull-up" (a goal people want) >> "Pinch: squeeze or hold?" (a question few ask).
- **Informative beats ad-like.** B (teaching) got 9 follows, C (felt like an ad) got 1, with more views.
- **Follows need a future.** One-off discussion reels (ranking) get comments but no follows. How-tos and series give a reason to follow.
- **Comments ≠ likes.** Debate formats pull comments, not likes. That's fine if comments are the goal.
- **Saves come from protocols.** "Save this" only works when there's a concrete plan (holes, %, sets). Opinion reels → end on a question instead.
- **First second must be readable.** Unknown device in split screen = swipe. Open on a recognisable action (climbing, pull-up).
- **Pace:** too fast feels "ADHD". Give each idea ~4–5 s, use bridges ("2 popular ways:") between parts.
- **Broad topics reach further.** Pull-ups/OAP reach fitness people too; crimps/slopers only climbers. Both have a place: broad for reach, niche for buyers.
- **Regular reels can go big too.** B2 hit 100k as a normal reel. Trial reels = testing tool, not a reach trick. Keep auto-share off.
- **A climbing hook clip alone doesn't fix the hook.** Pinch v6 (climb clip + radar zoom) held 54.5% past 3 s vs 58.6% for v2 without it, and got fewer saves (17 vs 31). The topic ("pinch: squeeze or hold?") is the limit, not the first frame.
- **TikTok doesn't carry over (yet).** B2 = 110k on Instagram, 78 views as a TikTok repost; all 4 reposts got 2–88 views, 0 shares. Reposting gives no TikTok distribution on this account so far.
- **A strong hook without a takeaway goes nowhere.** Ice axes v1/v2 held 67–69% past 3 s (PPS level) but got 1–2 saves, 0–2 shares and stalled at ~2k views. Elbow (67%) had the same hook rate and got 370 saves, 149 shares, 13k views. Hook gets the view; a protocol/problem gets the save and share that drive reach.
- **Problem + protocol works beyond PPS.** Elbow (no PPS, no milestone goal) = 2nd best reel ever. The recipe from 2b holds for a "pain/prevention" problem, not only for a goal.
- **Check rankings again at day 5–7.** Climbing ranking went 1.6k → 5.0k views between day 2 and day 5; how-tos peak faster.
- **Be honest in claims.** Only promise what we'll actually measure; say "our take", not "the only way".

### From watching others (Lattice, Nov 2026 – Pieterjan's observation)
- **Real ascents score best.** Real climbing on real walls beats everything else.
- **Explanation videos do less, but still well.**
- **Products and static shots don't score.**
- **Climbers with analytics drawn on the footage work very well** (numbers/lines on the climber). Fits our brand: measured strength.
- **Technique explained on the wall with a voice-over** works.

Implication for us: real footage of climbers + real numbers overlaid (testing station, Max Games, comps, Entralpi live kg) is our unique angle. Avoid product-only and static scenes.

## 2b. Starting point for every reel: goal → problem → product
Pieterjan's product question "which problem does this solve?" is also the reel's angle. PPS reel = goal (one-arm pull-up) + protocol → 101k views, ~31 sales. Elbow reel = problem (elbow pain) + protocol → 13.4k views, 370 saves.

| Goal | Problem | Product | Reel idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| Milestone skill (OAP, one-arm hang, lock-off) | step too big, no progress | PPS, One Arm Trainer, One Finger Trainer | "Your first one-arm hang in 4 steps" |
| **Train at home, little time** (priority) | no time for the gym (jobs, kids) | hangboards, bands, Entralpi, rings, PPS | series "20 min at home" |
| Trip prep (Font, Spain, Frankenjura) | "Font slopers destroy me" | Sloper King, pinch tools, Rolling Handles | "6 weeks until Font? Train this." |
| Weak hold type | crimps / slopers / pinches | pinch block, Thumb War, Sloper King, hangboards | "Can't hold slopers?" |
| Measure progress | "am I getting stronger?" | Entralpi, testing stations | "Same hang, 2 months later" |
| Stay injury-free, climb longer (35+, comeback) | body can't keep up with fingers | bands, Popeye Pronator | antagonist series |
| Multi-day trips | skin gone after day 1 | Repair balm | "Day 3: save your skin" |
| Technique / footwork | strong but clumsy | footwork panel, ice axes | foot swap, ice axes |
| Beat friends / compete | who's strongest? | testing stations, Max Games, monthly comps | comp reels from Jan 2027 |

Rules: product in shot early and named in the caption; training language (helps/builds), never fixes/cures; caption line "Pain already? See a physio" for body-related reels.

## 4. New content forms to try

### Humor
- **"Climber vs gym bro"** – same exercise, two attitudes (text overlays, one person, two takes).
- **"Things climbers say"**: "I'll just do one more attempt" → timelapse of 20 attempts.
- **Expectation vs reality**: "My training plan" (neat schedule) vs "My actual week" (chaos).
- **The forearm pump** – exaggerated reaction after a long hang, deadpan caption.

### Analytics overlays (high priority)
- **Testing station / Max Games**: climber pulls → live number + % bodyweight + rank among gym climbers drawn on the footage.
- **Entralpi hang**: live kg on the climber, target line, "this is 62% of bodyweight".
- **Ascent breakdown**: wall angle, hold type labels, time per move, hip path traced on the climber.
- **Two climbers, same boulder**: overlay where they differ (hips, reach, tempo).

### Cinematic / spectacular
- **Slow-motion dyno or cut-loose** on the shop wall (phone slo-mo, 120/240 fps).
- **Macro close-ups**: chalk off fingertips, skin on a sloper, the thumb closing a pinch. Little text, good music.
- **Weight-plate drama**: Loading Pin lift with a heavy stack, low angle, slow-mo drop.
- **Radar zoom** (have it) on any spectacular hold or body position.

### Confrontational hooks (honest, not ragebait)
- **"You can pull. But can you push?"** → antagonist test + why it matters for elbows/shoulders.
- **"Your fingers are strong. Your thumb isn't."** → pinch.
- **"You don't need more pull-ups."** → weighted / one-arm progression.
- **"Hangboarding at bodyweight is a beginner mistake."** → Entralpi, train at your level.
- **"Most climbers will never do a one-arm pull-up. Here's what it takes."**

Rule: say something we can defend in the comments.

## 5. Process per reel

1. Pick the idea and the hook (goal or problem, readable in second 1).
2. Film: vertical, tripod, tight on the action, same light; one hook clip with recognisable climbing/pulling.
3. Build → review → post (trial for new formats; regular for proven ones).
4. Log numbers here at ~72 h; one line on *why*.
