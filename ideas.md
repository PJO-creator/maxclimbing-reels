## 2. What we've learned so far

- **Hook rate tells the story.** All PPS reels hold 69–72% past 3 s; Entralpi 56%, rankings 46–55%. Start with a recognisable action + a goal.
- **Shares drive the big one.** B2 got 763 shares (others: 1–43). Its caption opened with "Send this to the friend who's been almost there for 2 years". Use a share prompt aimed at a specific friend.
- **Saves follow protocols.** B2 1,311 saves, B 69, C 62 vs rankings 2–4 and Entralpi 9.
- **Goal hooks beat method hooks.** "How to train for a one-arm pull-up" (a goal people want) >> "Pinch: squeeze or hold?" (a question few ask).
- **Informative beats ad-like.** B (teaching) got 9 follows, C (felt like an ad) got 1, with more views.
- **Follows need a future.** One-off discussion reels (ranking) get comments but no follows. How-tos and series give a reason to follow.
- **Comments ≠ likes.** Debate formats pull comments, not likes. That's fine if comments are the goal.
- **Saves come from protocols.** "Save this" only works when there's a concrete plan (holes, %, sets). Opinion reels → end on a question instead.
- **First second must be readable.** Unknown device in split screen = swipe. Open on a recognisable action (climbing, pull-up).
- **Pace:** too fast feels "ADHD". Give each idea ~4–5 s, use bridges ("2 popular ways:") between parts.
- **Broad topics reach further.** Pull-ups/OAP reach fitness people too; crimps/slopers only climbers. Both have a place: broad for reach, niche for buyers.
- **Regular reels can go big too.** B2 hit 100k as a normal reel. Trial reels = testing tool, not a reach trick. Keep auto-share off.
- **Be honest in claims.** Only promise what we'll actually measure; say "our take", not "the only way".

## 4. New content forms to try

### Humor
- **"Climber vs gym bro"** – same exercise, two attitudes (text overlays, one person, two takes).
- **"Things climbers say"**: "I'll just do one more attempt" → timelapse of 20 attempts.
- **Expectation vs reality**: "My training plan" (neat schedule) vs "My actual week" (chaos).
- **The forearm pump** – exaggerated reaction after a long hang, deadpan caption.

### Cinematic / spectacular
- **Slow-motion dyno or cut-loose** on the shop wall (phone slo-mo, 120/240 fps).
- **Macro close-ups**: chalk off fingertips, skin on a sloper, the thumb closing a pinch. Little text, good music.
- **Weight-plate drama**: Loading Pin lift with a heavy stack, low angle, slow-mo drop.
- **Radar zoom** (have it) on any spectacular hold or body position.

### Confrontational hooks (honest, not ragebait)
- **"You can pull. But can you push?"** → antagonist test + why it matters for elbows/shoulders.
- **"Your fingers are strong. Your thumb isn't."** → pinch.
- **"You don't need more pull-ups."** → weighted / one-arm progression.
- **"Hangboarding at bodyweight is a beginner mistake."** → Entralpi, train at your level.
- **"Most climbers will never do a one-arm pull-up. Here's what it takes."**

Rule: say something we can defend in the comments.

## 5. Process per reel

1. Pick the idea and the hook (goal or problem, readable in second 1).
2. Film: vertical, tripod, tight on the action, same light; one hook clip with recognisable climbing/pulling.
3. Build → review → post (trial for new formats; regular for proven ones).
4. Log numbers here at ~72 h; one line on *why*.
